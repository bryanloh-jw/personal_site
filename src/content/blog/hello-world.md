I've been meaning to write publicly for a while. Not in a "building my brand" sense, but more in the way you'd keep a notebook — because writing forces clarity, and clarity is usually worth having.

This site is simple by design. A few pages, a place for posts, nothing that gets in the way of the words. If it helps someone think through something they're working on, or prompts a good conversation, that's more than enough.

## What to expect

Mostly notes on software engineering — how I think about problems, things I've learned the hard way, tools and ideas that have changed how I work. Some of it will be technical. Some of it won't be.

I don't have a posting schedule. I'll write when I have something worth saying.
